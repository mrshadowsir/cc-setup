🔋 Battery restriction hatao …

Taaki phone lock ho ya app peeche chali jaye, tab bhi kaam chalta rahe.

Haath se karna ho to (steps)
  1. Settings > Apps > Termux > Battery > No restrictions
  2. Redmi/Xiaomi: Settings > Apps > Permissions > Autostart > Termux ON
  3. Recent apps me Termux card dabake pakdo > Lock
  4. Termux notification me "Wake lock" dikhna chahiye

Browser se buttons na khulein to Termux me likho: ccs battery
